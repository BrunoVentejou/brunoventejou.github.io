excerpt: ' We investigate the dynamics of a swimmer in a confined environment. Our findings show that the transition from the Stokes regime to the intermediate intertial regime can be tuned solely by adjusting the swimmers confinement. The crossover between the two is governed by the formation of a boundary layer and occurs when the confinement length scale becomes larger than the Blasius boundary layer thickness. We further support these results with an analytical study, which shows qualitative agreement with the numerical simulations.'
